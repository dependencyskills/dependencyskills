# Postmortems

Retrospectives on approaches that shipped and then failed, written after the fact by the people who shipped them. They are worth more than a rejected-alternatives list because the failure is inspectable: the published artifacts still exist and anyone can download one and look, so the account can be checked rather than taken on trust.

They are research like anything else in this section, so they continue the RAD numbering rather than starting a sequence of their own.
