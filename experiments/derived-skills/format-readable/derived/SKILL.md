# format-readable

The API lives in packages under `io.github.aughtone.readable` [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.functions.kt:1], not under the artifact's own name: the only thing in `io.github.aughtone.format.readable` is a generated resources accessor [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/format/readable/resources/Res.kt:7] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/format/readable/resources/Res.kt:17].

## What it solves

It says how long ago or how soon something is — "5 minutes ago", "in 2 hours", "Yesterday", "Tomorrow", "just now" — for an `Instant`, a `LocalDateTime`, a `LocalDate` or a `LocalTime`, and switches to a plain date or time once the gap is large [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.extensions.kt:48] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.extensions.kt:71] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.extensions.kt:167] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.extensions.kt:235] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.extensions.kt:310] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeTime.resources.kt:178] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/relative/RelativeDirection.kt:13]. It states a length of time as one rounded unit, "2 hours" or "5m" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/duration/Duration.functions.kt:12] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/duration/Duration.resources.kt:116]. It prints numbers with the locale's thousands and decimal separators [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.functions.kt:11], shortens big counts to "1.5k" or "1M" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.functions.kt:184] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.functions.kt:185], turns byte counts into sizes such as "1.5 KiB" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/metrics/DataSize.functions.kt:11], and scales metres, grams, watts and similar to "1.5 km" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/metrics/Metric.functions.kt:41]. It writes ordinals such as "1st" and "2e" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/ordinality/Ordinality.functions.kt:10] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/ordinality/Ordinality.functions.kt:11], joins strings into "Monday, Wednesday, and Friday" or "red or green" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/list/List.functions.kt:7] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/list/List.functions.kt:28], and puts a currency symbol before or after an amount as the locale expects [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/financial/Money.functions.kt:9]. It prints coordinates as decimal degrees or degrees-minutes-seconds with a hemisphere letter [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/quantitative/Quantitative.functions.kt:45] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/quantitative/Quantitative.functions.kt:46], a compass bearing as "90° (E)" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/quantitative/Quantitative.functions.kt:28], and a time zone as an abbreviation such as "EST" or as its full name [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/time/TimeZone.extensions.kt:14] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/time/TimeZone.extensions.kt:23]. It also exposes the plural-category rule it uses — zero, one, two, few, many, other — for a count [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/PluralCategory.kt:8] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/PluralCategory.kt:32], and a set of ready-made `Locale` constants [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/Locales.kt:8].

## Invariants and traps

**Decimal places are a ceiling, not a fixed width.** `precision` rounds to at most that many places and a zero fraction is dropped [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:19] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:30] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:38]. `2.0.formatReadable()` is "2", not "2.0", and a size of exactly 1024 bytes prints "1 KiB" although that function's doc comment shows "1.0 KiB" [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/metrics/DataSize.functions.kt:13] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/metrics/DataSize.functions.kt:61]. Money goes through the same formatter with the currency's digit count as the precision [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/financial/Money.resources.kt:88] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/financial/Money.resources.kt:90], so twelve and a half prints "12.5" and a whole amount "12" — never padded to "12.50" [uncited]. Symptom: prices missing their trailing zero.

**A negative number whose whole part has 3, 6 or 9 digits gets a separator after the minus sign.** Grouping reverses the integer text, sign included, and cuts it in threes [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:25] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:26] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:27] [src: commonMain/io/github/aughtone/readable/number/Number.resources.kt:28]. So `(-123).formatReadable(Locales.English)` is "-,123" and −123456 is "-,123,456", while −1234 is right [uncited].
